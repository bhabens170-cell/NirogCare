# 🩺 NirogCare – Your Personal Mobile Health Companion

NirogCare is a bilingual *(Hindi + English)* open-source mobile healthcare application designed to make everyday health guidance **simple, structured, and accessible to everyone**.

The goal is to bring essential health tools to users in one place — from symptom guidance to wellness tracking — even for those who prefer regional languages.

---

## 🌟 Features

- 📍 Bilingual Support (Hindi + English UI)
- 🤖 AI-assisted symptom guidance *(expandable with online or local models)*
- 🧠 Condition–based suggestions for daily health issues
- 📂 Organized sections for Men’s, Women’s, Kids & General Health
- 🔒 Local data storage without cloud dependency
- 🧬 Extendable medical knowledge modules
- 🧾 Clean, accessible UI for all age groups

> Future upgrades planned:  
> pregnancy tracking, period cycle prediction, OCR for prescriptions, lab report analysis, reminders, emergency guidance & more.
